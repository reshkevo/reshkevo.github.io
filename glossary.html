Forex glossary

Plain-English definitions of the terms you will meet in the course and on your broker platform.

Advertisement
Ask
The price at which you can buy a currency pair.
Base currency
The first currency in a pair; the one you are buying or selling.
Bid
The price at which you can sell a currency pair.
Candlestick
A chart bar showing open, high, low and close for one period.
CFD
Contract for difference: a leveraged derivative that tracks an asset's price without owning it.
Drawdown
The decline in account value from a peak to a trough.
EMA
Exponential moving average: an average that weights recent prices more heavily.
Equity
Account balance plus or minus open profit and loss.
Fundamental analysis
Analysing economic data and policy to judge a currency's value.
Hedge
A position opened to offset risk in another position.
Leverage
Using borrowed capital from a broker to control a larger position than your deposit alone allows.
Liquidity
How easily an asset can be traded without moving its price.
Lot
A standardized trade size: standard (100,000), mini (10,000) or micro (1,000) units.
Margin
The portion of your account set aside to keep a leveraged position open.
Margin call
A broker warning that equity is nearing the margin required for open trades.
Pip
The standard unit of price movement: usually the 4th decimal place, or the 2nd for JPY pairs.
Pipette
One tenth of a pip.
Position size
How many lots or units you trade on a given position.
Quote currency
The second currency in a pair; the one the price is expressed in.
Resistance
A price zone where selling has previously stopped a rally.
Risk-reward ratio
The potential loss compared with the potential gain on a trade.
RSI
Relative Strength Index: a 0 to 100 momentum oscillator.
Slippage
The difference between the expected and actual execution price.
Spread
The difference between the bid and ask price; a cost of trading.
Stop-loss
A pre-set order that closes a trade at a specified loss level.
Stop-out
Automatic closing of trades by the broker when equity falls too far.
Support
A price zone where buying has previously stopped a decline.
Swap
An interest charge or credit for holding a position overnight.
Take-profit
A pre-set order that closes a trade at a specified profit level.
Volatility
How much and how fast price moves.
