Support and resistance retest strategy

Wait for a level to break, then trade the retest of the broken level instead of chasing the breakout.

7 min readStrategies
Advertisement

The idea

When resistance breaks decisively it often becomes support, and vice versa. Entering on the retest gives you a defined level to place your stop against rather than buying a spike.

The rules

  1. Mark levels on a higher timeframe: zones where price reacted at least twice.
  2. Wait for a decisive break: a strong candle that closes beyond the zone, not just a wick.
  3. Wait for the retest: price returns to the broken zone.
  4. Confirm: a rejection candle holds the zone from the new side.
  5. Stop: beyond the zone, with a little room for noise.
  6. Target: the next significant level, minimum 1:2.

Things that make it better

  • Break and retest in the direction of the higher-timeframe trend
  • Avoid entries right before high-impact news
  • Skip it if the retest never comes. Do not chase
Failed breakouts

Many breaks fail and reverse. That is why you wait for confirmation, keep the stop tight to the zone and size the trade so a stop-out is a normal 1% cost.
