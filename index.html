The rule we do not break

PHILOSOPHY
01

Position size before entry

You decide how much you can lose before you decide how much you want to make. Size is set from your stop, never the other way round.

02

The stop-loss is not optional

Every position ships with an exit already placed. No clean invalidation point means no setup, just a hope.

03

One bad week cannot end you

We cap risk per trade and per week so a losing streak is an inconvenience, not an extinction event.
