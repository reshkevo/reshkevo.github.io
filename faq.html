Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about the academy, risk and getting started.

Is Reshkevo FX free?

Yes. The course, the risk calculator, the guides and the community groups are free to use.

Do you give signals?

We discuss trade ideas and risk decisions openly in the community, but no signal replaces your own risk management. Nothing here is financial advice.

How much money do I need to start?

You can start with a demo account and $0. For a live account, only use money you can afford to lose, and size positions to your balance with the risk calculator.

What risk per trade do you recommend?

Many risk-first traders risk 1 to 2% of their account per trade or less. The right number is personal and depends on your plan, but the principle is to cap it in advance.

Can I really practise with $10,000 virtual funds?

Yes. Our partner broker Exness offers demo accounts with virtual funds so you can practise without risking real money. See the demo account guide for the link and what to practise.

Is forex trading safe?

Forex and CFD trading carries a high level of risk and can result in loss of your capital. Most retail traders lose money. Education, strict risk management and realistic expectations improve your odds but never remove the risk.

Which timeframe should I trade?

It depends on your schedule and temperament. Many beginners prefer H1 to H4 charts, checking the daily chart for context.

When are the best times to trade from Kenya?

The London session and the London to New York overlap, roughly mid-morning to evening in East Africa Time. See the sessions guide.

Which pairs should a beginner trade?

Major pairs such as EUR/USD and GBP/USD have the tightest spreads and deepest liquidity, which makes them common starting points.

How do I join the community?

Join the WhatsApp or Telegram group from the community page, or message us directly on WhatsApp.
