Rule 01, made practical

Work out your position size before you place the trade.

The one tool every member is told to use before every entry. Fill in your account and your stop: it tells you the size, not the direction.

How it works

The calculator uses the formula from Lesson 4:

Risk amount = Balance × Risk %
Lots = Risk amount ÷ (Stop pips × Pip value)

Example: $1,000 account, 1% risk, 25-pip stop on EUR/USD gives 0.04 lots. Change the stop and the size changes with it, so your dollar risk stays constant.

Remember

The calculator tells you how much to trade, never whether to trade. Place the stop where your idea is invalidated, then size to fit.
