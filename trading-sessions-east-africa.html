Forex trading sessions in East Africa Time (EAT)

When Sydney, Tokyo, London and New York actually open, translated into Nairobi time.

5 min readMarket basics
Advertisement

The four sessions in EAT

East Africa Time is UTC+3 all year with no daylight saving. London and New York shift by an hour with their own daylight saving, so the hours below are approximate ranges.

SessionApprox. hours (EAT)Character
Sydney00:00 – 09:00Quiet; AUD and NZD pairs
Tokyo03:00 – 12:00JPY and Asia-Pacific pairs; often range-bound
London10:00/11:00 – 19:00/20:00Busiest session; strong moves on EUR and GBP pairs
New York15:00/16:00 – 00:00High volume; US data releases; overlaps London

The overlaps that matter

  • Tokyo + London (about 10:00–12:00 EAT): a short window where Asian trends meet European liquidity.
  • London + New York (about 15:00–19:00 EAT): usually the highest-volume stretch of the day, with tighter spreads and faster moves on the majors.
Match the session to the pair

EUR/USD and GBP/USD tend to be most active during London and New York. USD/JPY is active in Tokyo and New York. AUD and NZD pairs tend to wake up in Sydney and Tokyo.

A practical routine for Nairobi-based traders

  1. Check the economic calendar in the morning for the day's high-impact releases.
  2. Mark daily support and resistance before London opens.
  3. Trade your plan during the London session or the London–New York overlap.
  4. Avoid opening new trades in the minutes around major news.
  5. Log everything in your journal at the end of the session.

Always confirm exact session hours on your broker's platform, since they can shift with daylight saving in Europe and the US.
